FAQ

Car detailing FAQ for Perth drivers

What is included in a full car detail?

Our Platinum package includes a full interior and exterior detail, covering the wash stages, wheels, arches, glass, engine bay, vacuuming, interior wipe-down and trim finishing for a complete refresh.

Do you offer interior-only car detailing?

Yes. Our Interior Only package is ideal for drivers who want the cabin restored and professionally cleaned without booking an exterior detail at the same time.

What is included in the Pre-Sale package?

Our Pre-Sale package includes the full interior and exterior detail, then adds a clay bar treatment and Stage 1 machine buff and polish to improve gloss and presentation before sale or handover.

How long does a car detail take?

Detailing times vary depending on the package, vehicle size and overall condition. Once we know what you are booking in for, we can give you a realistic timeframe.

Can you detail SUVs, utes and prestige vehicles?

Absolutely. We work on a wide range of vehicles across Perth, including everyday family cars, European vehicles, performance cars, SUVs and utes.

Will detailing improve the resale presentation of my car?

Yes. A proper detail can make a big difference to gloss, presentation and overall impression, which is especially helpful if you are preparing the vehicle for sale or trade-in.
