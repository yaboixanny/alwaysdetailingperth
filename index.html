FAQ

Ceramic coating FAQ for Perth drivers

Is ceramic coating worth it on a brand new car?

Yes, especially if you want to protect the finish before daily driving, UV exposure, and wash marring start to take a toll. A new car is often the best time to coat because the paint usually needs less correction.

Is dealer paint protection the same as ceramic coating?

Not always. Dealer paint protection packages vary a lot in quality, prep, and durability. A dedicated ceramic coating service generally includes proper inspection, paint prep, and a more tailored application process.

Do new cars need paint correction?

Quite often, yes. Even brand new cars can arrive with light swirl marks, wash marring, transport marks, or minor dealership prep damage. A quick assessment tells us how much correction is actually worthwhile.

How long does ceramic coating last?

That depends on the coating chosen, how the vehicle is used, and how it’s maintained. With proper care, a quality ceramic coating can offer protection and gloss benefits for years rather than months.

How long does the process take?

Most ceramic coating jobs take at least one to several days depending on the package, the condition of the paint, and curing requirements. We’ll give you a realistic timeframe before booking the car in.

Will ceramic coating prevent scratches?

No coating makes paint scratch-proof. Ceramic coating helps with chemical resistance, gloss retention, and wash maintenance, but it won’t stop stone chips or deeper scratches from poor washing or contact.

Does ceramic coating make the car easier to clean?

Yes. One of the biggest benefits is easier maintenance. Dirt, water, and grime release more easily, which means washing is simpler and the car tends to stay looking cleaner for longer.

Can I bring my car straight from the dealer?

Absolutely. Many clients bring their cars in straight after collection so we can inspect the paint, correct any delivery marks if needed, and coat it before it goes through months of regular use.

Why choose Always Detailing over the dealer?

We focus specifically on preparation, product choice, and finish quality rather than bundling protection into a quick handover process. You get a more personalised service and clearer guidance on what your car actually needs.

How much does new car ceramic coating cost?

Our ceramic coating packages start from $999, with pricing depending on the package selected, vehicle size, and any paint correction required. We’re happy to recommend the best fit after a quick chat.

Do you offer warranty on ceramic coating?

Warranty options depend on the coating package and aftercare requirements. We’ll explain what is covered, what maintenance is expected, and how to keep your protection performing as intended.
